Offering Summary

MGR Real Estate is pleased to present 6181 Joshua Palmer Way, Banning — an approximately 5.99-acre (260,923 SF) vacant commercial development opportunity consisting of four parcels, strategically positioned adjacent to the Interstate 10 corridor near N. Highland Springs Avenue.

The property is zoned General Commercial (GC) by the City of Banning, providing flexibility for a broad range of potential commercial and service-oriented development concepts, subject to applicable City approvals and development standards. The site sits within the rapidly developing Banning–Beaumont corridor, near San Gorgonio Memorial Hospital, Sundance Corporate Center, and expanding residential communities — a strong mix of nearby commercial and residential demand generators. The seller may consider seller financing with acceptable terms.
