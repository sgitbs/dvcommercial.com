Offering Summary

A rare heavy-manufacturing opportunity built around an exceptional power infrastructure: 170,000 SF across a main building, attached structure, and separate steel buildings, on 21.5 acres zoned Heavy Manufacturing (HM).

The facility includes 10,000 SF of executive offices, 5,000 SF of engineering offices, and a 10,000 SF bonus mezzanine office (not included in total SF). A Union Pacific rail spur extends into the yard and building, accommodating two railcars inside the warehouse and seven in the exterior rail yard.
