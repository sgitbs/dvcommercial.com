Industrial case study

$26M Riverside industrial sale-leaseback.

The property owner needed to unlock capital from the real estate while continuing to operate its solar-related business from the facility. Daniel sourced an investment group seeking industrial real estate without an immediate possession requirement.

The sale-leaseback enabled the seller to monetize the asset and begin a triple-net lease immediately, while the buyer received income-producing industrial real estate from day one. The structure provided an operating transition period and aligned both parties’ objectives.

Seller strategy: Unlock equity → Maintain operations → Lease back the facility → Execute a future exit strategy.

Buyer strategy: Acquire institutional industrial asset → Receive immediate NNN income → Pursue long-term ownership.
