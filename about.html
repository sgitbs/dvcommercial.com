About Daniel

Commercial real estate strategy, built around the outcome.

Daniel Valenzuela advises investors, property owners, and operating businesses through acquisitions, dispositions, leasing, and investment decisions across Southern California and the Inland Empire.

His work spans transaction leadership and advisory across commercial real estate activity approaching $500M — from a $26,000,000 industrial sale-leaseback structured around a seller's ongoing operations, to a neighborhood retail sale repositioned through patient negotiation and competitive marketing, to owner-user and 1031 exchange transactions for individual investors.

Daniel's approach starts with the objective, not the listing. Before a property goes to market — or before an acquisition search begins — he works to understand what a client actually needs the transaction to accomplish: unlocking capital while preserving operations, positioning an asset for a stronger exit, or finding the right structure for a 1031 exchange. The strategy is built around that goal, then executed.
