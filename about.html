About Daniel

Strategy first.

Sr. Commercial Associate at MGR Real Estate, working with investors, property owners and business operators across Southern California's commercial real estate markets.

Daniel Valenzuela
Sr. Commercial Associate

Daniel Valenzuela approaches commercial real estate with a focus on strategy, negotiation and long-term client objectives. His experience spans acquisitions, dispositions and leasing assignments involving a broad range of commercial properties and clients.

From business owners evaluating their first owner-user property to investors and property owners navigating larger commercial opportunities, Daniel's objective remains consistent: understand the opportunity, structure the strategy and execute with purpose.

Every engagement starts the same way — with the client's objective, not the listing. Whether the goal is acquiring a first owner-user building, disposing of a legacy asset, structuring a lease that supports an operating business, or positioning a property for its next use, Daniel works to understand what success actually looks like for that client before recommending a direction.

That process typically means evaluating the property itself, the market it sits in, the financial structure behind the decision, and the timeline the client is working against — then building a strategy around those specifics rather than defaulting to a one-size-fits-all approach. Negotiation is treated as a structured part of that strategy, not an afterthought once an offer is on the table.

Clients working with Daniel are typically involved directly with him throughout a transaction, from initial evaluation through closing — commercial real estate decisions carry enough complexity, and enough consequence, that they deserve direct, informed representation at every stage.

"Understand the opportunity, structure the strategy, execute with purpose."

Daniel's experience reflects leadership across nearly $500 million in commercial real estate acquisitions, dispositions and leasing transactions* — activity that spans owner-user, investment, retail, industrial, office and mixed commercial assets throughout the Inland Empire, Los Angeles County, Orange County and the broader Southern California market.

*Experience contributing to and leading commercial real estate transactions approaching $500 million in aggregate transaction activity across acquisitions, dispositions and leasing.
