Offering Summary

9517 Commerce Way presents a unique opportunity to acquire a lender-owned industrial facility in Adelanto, California. The property consists of approximately 20,164 SF of improvements on approximately 1.61 acres and is well suited for an owner-user seeking functional warehouse and industrial space with immediate occupancy potential.

Built in 2007, the building features approximately 22-foot clear ceiling height, two dock-high loading positions, single-tenant configuration, and ample on-site parking — flexible for warehouse, distribution, manufacturing, storage, and other industrial-related operations, subject to applicable zoning and municipal approvals. The property is currently vacant, allowing a purchaser to occupy the facility without accommodating an existing tenant or lease obligation. Positioned near established industrial uses and the Southern California Logistics Airport in the broader Victor Valley industrial region.
