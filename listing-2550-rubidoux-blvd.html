Offering Summary

A unique investment opportunity consisting of four contiguous parcels totaling approximately 4.68 acres, strategically located with excellent access to both the I-10 and SR-60 Freeways. The property currently features a two-story retail/commercial building and a freestanding storage warehouse, providing immediate functionality while also offering significant value-add and redevelopment potential.

With its M-SC (Manufacturing–Service Commercial) zoning designation, the property presents an outstanding opportunity for industrial development, including warehouses, manufacturing facilities, distribution, and service commercial operations, subject to City approval where applicable. Buyers may purchase all four parcels, or the seller will consider selling one-half of the property (North or South portion, each combining two parcels totaling approximately 2.34 acres).
