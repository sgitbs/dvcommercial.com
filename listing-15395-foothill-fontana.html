Offering Summary

A turn-key restaurant opportunity along Fontana's Foothill Boulevard / historic Route 66 corridor. The ±5,200 SF freestanding restaurant provides existing kitchen infrastructure, full bar, dining areas, private event rooms, strong visibility, prominent frontage, and convenient parking. Qualified operators may also pursue a lease-with-option-to-purchase structure.

The offering extends beyond the restaurant building. The ±40,844 SF commercial site includes approximately 7,806 SF of existing improvements plus an approximately 282 SF basement — the restaurant, a single-family residence, a freestanding studio apartment, a detached four-car garage, storage improvements, and additional site area. Its Route 66 identity, corner visibility, flexible existing improvements, parking, and meaningful land area create multiple paths for an investor, owner-user, operator, or redevelopment-focused buyer.
